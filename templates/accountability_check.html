How this works:
When a student submits anonymously, their user_id is not stored. Instead, a SHA-256 hash of their Enrollment Number + a secret pepper is saved as a crypto_token. To check if a specific student submitted malicious complaints, enter their Enrollment Number below. The system will hash it the same way and check for matches — without ever exposing the real identity in any query. This check is permanently logged in the audit trail.
